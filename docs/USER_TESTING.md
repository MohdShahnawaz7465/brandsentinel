## What we asked

Each person was given the link (https://brandsentinel-app.vercel.app) and about five minutes:

1. Press **Writer** under "try the demo".
2. In Review, pick a sample draft (or paste 2 or 3 lines of your own) and press **Run check**.
3. Apply one fix.

Then four questions:

- **A.** Did you understand what the tool does? (yes / a little / no)
- **B.** Where did you get stuck or confused?
- **C.** How useful were the findings and fixes? (1 to 5)
- **D.** What is the one thing you would change?
